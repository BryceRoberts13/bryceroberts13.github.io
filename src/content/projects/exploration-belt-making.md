Exploration track for **belts**: material weight, taper, hole spacing and wear, keeper design, and hardware choice. Goal is to build intuition on what holds up vs what looks clean before scaling to a main belt or a gear-style layout with extra carry.

**Focus**

- Strap geometry and length / hole pattern for real use (not only display)
- Edge creasing, burnishing, and thread if doing stitched construction
- Buckle types and how they change strap thickness and bend radius

**Room to fill later**

- Pattern notes and leather weight chosen
- Photos of prototypes and final belt
- What failed and what you’d repeat
