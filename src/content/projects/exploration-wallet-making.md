Exploration track for **wallets**: internal layout (card count, cash, ID visibility), thickness stack-up, and stitch vs fold-only approaches. Fits the “remake my wallet” goal without pretending the first pass is the last.

**Focus**

- Slot depth and stagger so cards slide without binding
- Lining or flesh side handling for abrasion
- Corners, creases, and keeping the silhouette thin in-pocket
